About

A founder, rooted in family

Richard Giaccio is managing Giaccio Properties, a family business specializing in custom homes throughout Westchester County, New York. Today he is President of Prime Payments, a Katonah-based merchant services company, and serves as Vice President of the Lewisboro Chamber of Commerce.

Richard carries forward Giaccio Properties with every home delivered to the standard of someone who puts his own name on it. A graduate of Western Connecticut State University, he has spent his career in Westchester County.
